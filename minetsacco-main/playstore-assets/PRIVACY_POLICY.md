# Privacy Policy for Minet SACCO Member Portal

**Last Updated:** August 12, 2026

---

## Introduction

Minet SACCO ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Minet SACCO Member Portal mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

When you register and use the App, we may collect personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

- **Member Identification:** Employee ID, member number
- **Contact Information:** Email address, phone number
- **Authentication Credentials:** Username and encrypted password
- **Financial Information:** Account balances, transaction history, loan details, savings records
- **Guarantorship Information:** Guarantor relationships and approval records

### 2. Automatically Collected Information

When you access the App, we may automatically collect:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** App features used, time spent, interaction patterns
- **Log Data:** IP address, access times, app crashes, error reports
- **Network Information:** Internet connection type and status

### 3. Push Notification Data

If you enable push notifications:

- **Notification Preferences:** Your opt-in/opt-out choices
- **Device Token:** Unique identifier for sending notifications
- **Notification Interaction:** Whether you opened or dismissed notifications

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

### Primary Purposes

- **Account Management:** Provide access to your SACCO member account
- **Loan Processing:** Process loan applications, eligibility checks, and approvals
- **Transaction Processing:** Record and display savings, deposits, and payments
- **Guarantorship:** Facilitate guarantor requests and approvals
- **Financial Reporting:** Generate account statements and reports
- **Authentication:** Verify your identity and secure your account

### Secondary Purposes

- **Notifications:** Send important alerts about account activities
- **Customer Support:** Respond to inquiries and provide assistance
- **Service Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns to enhance the App
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
- **Compliance:** Meet legal and regulatory requirements

---

## Data Storage and Security

### Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Authentication:** Secure login with encrypted password storage
- **Session Management:** Automatic session timeout for inactivity
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access to ensure data privacy
- **Secure Servers:** Data stored on secure servers with restricted access
- **No Local Storage:** Sensitive credentials are never stored on your device

### Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data retained while your membership is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Retained per SACCO policies and legal requirements
- **Transaction Records:** Maintained for audit and compliance (typically 7 years)
- **Deleted Accounts:** Securely deleted after retention period expires

---

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We DO NOT:

- ❌ Sell your personal information to third parties
- ❌ Share your data with advertisers or marketers
- ❌ Use your information for purposes unrelated to SACCO services
- ❌ Disclose your financial information publicly

### We MAY Share Information With:

- **SACCO Officials:** Authorized staff for account management and loan processing
- **Service Providers:** Technology partners who assist in app operation (under strict confidentiality)
- **Legal Authorities:** When required by law, court order, or government request
- **Auditors:** For compliance and audit purposes (anonymized when possible)

### Member Data Visibility

- **Your Data:** You can view only your own account information
- **Guarantor Requests:** Other members may see limited information when requesting you as a guarantor
- **SACCO Staff:** Authorized staff can access member data for official duties only

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control

You have the right to:

- **Access:** View all your personal and financial information in the App
- **Update:** Modify your contact information and preferences
- **Opt-Out:** Disable push notifications at any time
- **Request Deletion:** Contact us to request account data deletion
- **Data Portability:** Request a copy of your data in a portable format

### Managing Notifications

You can control push notifications:

- In-app settings: Disable in the App's notification settings
- Device settings: Disable in your device's app notification settings
